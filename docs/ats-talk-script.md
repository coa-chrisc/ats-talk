1. Four lessons from the course they can repeat in a meeting, and the sense that they understood each one.
2. An accurate picture of where the City stands: where it looks like it is on paper, where it is once its own work is counted and the outside recognition it already has, and where it could be with organization and a budget line. The program organizes an existing community of practitioners.

## The story

Two people went to a course and came back with four lessons. Before teaching the lessons, they show what the lessons answer: enterprise has moved to agents, spent heavily, changed how it reviews work, and stumbled wherever a limit was missing. Then the four lessons, one at a time. Then the City: it has not established a formal AI program, while its staff already have experience, outside recognition, infrastructure, and a working agent fleet. Then what to do first, starting with meetings and staff coordination. Then what to have ready when agents arrive anyway, who decides what, and the one line that must hold: AI helps staff serve residents while people retain responsibility for decisions about them. The close reveals that the plan was the four lessons applied, and that City staff have already done much of the groundwork.

**You say:** Writing a rule in an agent's instructions is different from enforcing it in the system. Consider an agent asked to delete a record.
