# Story plan for script v3

Written 2026-10-20 from the owner's review of the built v2 deck. This is the brief for rewriting `ats-talk-script.md`; nothing here is on screen.

## Diagnosis of v2

1. No setup, so no payoff. The course ideas appear one at a time with no frame; the room cannot count them, cannot expect them to return, and cannot notice at the end that the plan was those ideas applied.
2. Current state before the lens. The track comes before the ideas, so "behind" is judged by feel. Ideas first, then the City measured against each.
3. The enterprise picture is scattered (17 percent on the City slide, buy/boost/build on the talent slide, cancellations and incidents four slides apart).
4. Nothing is defined: agent, fleet, sandbox, Foundry, landing zone, levels.
5. The infrastructure story is one crowded slide doing five jobs. Ready infrastructure, the ideas mapping onto it, and the teachable development system are three slides.
6. The plan skips the AI program team.
7. Leaps at 4a→5, 8→9, 12→13, 15→16.
8. The asks read as a request list; each is a yes to something already in motion and the slide does not say so.

## Copy rules added for v3

- Titles of the form "X, and Y." at most one in five.
- "The course says" at most twice in the deck. Findings are stated as ours.
- No reassurance or self-commentary ("that is fine", "that is the point", "the honest position", "the most reassuring slide", "here is the shift in one line", "deliberately modest").
- No aphorism endings ("most of it moved badly", "not out of the fight", "that is the answer"). One per deck at most.
- No caption that restates its tag.
- Every part ends with a handoff: the last story line of its last slide names what comes next.
- The four ideas are the spine: numbered on slide 2, one slide each in Part 3, checked against the City on slide 14, returned on slide 28.

Part 2. Where enterprise is

Part 3. The four ideas

8. Enforced rules, scaled to risk (stair and dial, as built).

Part 4. Where the City stands

Part 6. Ready, the ask, the reveal
